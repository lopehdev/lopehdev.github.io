03 / ABOUT

Gameplay first.

I build gameplay experiences by bringing together systems, enemy behaviour and player interaction.

I'm Lopeh, a game developer focused on Gameplay Programming and Game Design. I work primarily in Unreal Engine with C++ and Blueprints, and I enjoy shaping mechanics from their underlying logic through to how they feel in play.

On Archangel: Demon Rush, I worked on the enemy AI for three distinct demons, including their Behaviour Trees, Animation Blueprints and enemy Blueprints.
